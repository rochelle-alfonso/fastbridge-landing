Seven hover treatments for the How It Works steps. Step 01 is active — hover steps 02 and 03 to compare. Click any step to switch the active state.

01

Opacity lift

Inactive steps brighten to full opacity — minimal, lets the dimmed state do the work.

02

Underline preview

Blue underline sweeps in from the left — previews the active state without switching step.

03 · Live on landing

Number grow

The background number scales up and brightens — echoes the active/inactive size transition.

04

Content nudge

Label, heading, and description shift 6px right — subtle directional cue.

05

Grey fill rise

Same fill-rise as your buttons — grey background rises from the bottom of the step block.

06

Blue heading

Heading tints brand blue on hover — quick scan affordance without layout shift.

07 · Recommended

Combo — opacity + underline + blue heading

Layers three subtle cues together. Grey underline on hover, soft blue at full width.
