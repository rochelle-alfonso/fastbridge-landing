Button hover options

Animated landing Production

Eight hover treatments for the hero CTAs. Hover each pair on the blue gradient stage. Option 05 matches what’s live on the landing today.

01

Lift + shadow

Rises 2px with a soft drop shadow. Clean, familiar, works on both button styles.

02

Soft scale

Grows to 104% in place — no vertical movement. Feels tactile without floating.

03

Glow ring

Outer halo on hover — white glow on Bridge Now, blue-tinted on the glass button.

04

Shimmer sweep

A light band sweeps across on hover — premium feel, subtle on the glass variant.

05 Live on landing

Fill rise

Background fills from bottom — white button inverts to dark on hover.

06

Arrow nudge

A → fades in and the label shifts — signals forward motion / “go”.

07

Spring pop

Bouncy overshoot scale — playful, slightly more energetic than soft scale.

08

Border draw

Border thickens inward on hover — minimal, editorial. No movement.
